# PROPOSED LORE (not canon)

**Status: PROPOSED. Nothing in this file is established. It is awaiting your approval.**
The game does not use any *rules* from this document; where the Deck A slice contains new
*details* (below), they are tagged `canon: "proposed"` in data and listed here so you can accept,
change or strike each one. Nothing here resolves an unresolved mystery (who/what made the Keys,
the purpose of the Great Red Spot station, what O. Algo is).

## 0. Correction about "Section 4"
Earlier in the project I reported that Section 4 of the Lore Bible PDF was blank. That was wrong.
Section 4 ("Antagonists & Worldbuilding") has content: the O. Algo anomaly, the three Key classes,
the environmental hazards and their diagram. The "None" line I saw is a stray empty code block
from the Google Docs export. What the Lore Bible genuinely **does not give** is:

1. a description of the **Zero-G hazard** (it appears in the hazard diagram but is never described),
2. the **physical layout of the ship** (decks, room names, where things are),
3. where the **lead-lined bulkhead chambers** are,
4. how the **Flight Deck** (where the October 15 blunder happens) relates to the **armored bridge deck**.

## 1. Zero-G hazard (PROPOSAL, not implemented anywhere)
- **Name:** Gravity Fault. A section where artificial gravity has failed, locally and unpredictably.
- **Cause:** deliberately unstated. The ship's logs only record "gravity plating irregular"; whether
  O. Algo, the Keys, or damage is responsible stays open.
- **Behaviour (gameplay):** the player floats; movement becomes drift along the last direction of travel;
  interaction needs a handhold. Loose items (including pickups) drift. The effect is confined to marked
  sections and never affects the whole ship.
- **Counterplay:** hard-surface handholds and rails; the quartermaster's *Gravity Tether & Barrier*
  anchors a player (and in co-op, other players) so they can cross a fault safely. Faults can be
  escaped by walking out of the marked area, never by timers.
- **Presentation:** warning stripes that strobe; debris and loose paper drifting in the air; muffled,
  low-passed ambience; PA announcement ("gravity plating irregular").
- **Interaction with other hazards:** pairs with Decompression Corridors (drifting toward open vacuum
  ports is the danger); never combined with Radiation Surges in the same room.
- **Decisions needed:** approve / change the behaviour above; confirm the cause stays unstated.

## 3. New environmental details added in Deck A (all `canon: "proposed"`)
These are small, mundane or atmospheric. None is a historical event beyond what the Lore Bible states.
- A **live-stream / sponsorship** motif: "ON AIR" sign, a camera on a tripod aimed at the command chair,
  "SPONSORED FLIGHT" banner, a crew cassette of live-stream outtakes (the Lore Bible says the mission was
  a sponsored live-streamed trip; the specific props are new).
- **Flight deck log** and **black-box log #0811-A**: the Lore Bible has the October 15 incident and a
  black-box transcript; the game's versions are *adaptations in the stand-in characters' voices*, not a
  reproduction, and the exact wording is new.
- **Crew notes** (one per stand-in character), a **supply manifest**, an **escape pod provisioning
  checklist** with unconfirmed assignments (no assignment is stated, on purpose), an **engineering
  maintenance log**, and a **corrupted incident report** (text eaten by symbols, consistent with the
  Lore Bible's O. Algo behaviour).
- **Key presence:** small crystalline-metal pyramid shards growing out of a pried-open vent and on the floor
  of Section B. Visual only; no explanation, no behaviour.
- **Sealed Engine Core door**, locked behind "override credentials" with no source named.
- **Ship's system voice** at the bridge terminal (status reports; unlocks Section B).
- A **PA system** with generic warnings and no voice acting.

## 4. Lead-lined bulkhead chambers (PROPOSAL, not built)
Marked with a radiation trefoil and a heavy door, placed so no habitable area is more than about one
minute from one. In the slice's footprint the proposed spots would be: off the main corridor between the
Supply Room and Section B, and inside the Command Bridge's rear wall.
